About Me

Hi!! This is Tiasha. I am a data and research professional interested in the intersection of AI, analytics, language, and human behavior. My study focuses on NLP and computational linguistics, particularly Bengali language technologies, with recent work on explainable sentiment analysis and semantic opacity in Bengali idioms.

I am especially interested in how generative AI, language models, and automated translation are reshaping economic and social outcomes. My long-term goal is to build an interdisciplinary career combining quantitative methods, technology, business and social science to study the broader impact of AI.
